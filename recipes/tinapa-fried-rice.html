Tinapa Fried Rice

The fastest way to turn last night's rice into something you actually look forward to. One piece of smoked fish flavors the whole pan.

Cost per serving ₱22
Total cost ₱44
Serves 2
Time 15 min

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice, day-old3 cups
  • Tinapa (smoked fish), flaked1 piece
  • Garlic, minced3 cloves
  • Egg2
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Spring onion, sliced2 stalks
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Calamansi2 pieces, optional
Budget swap No tinapa in the fridge? Dried dilis (anchovies) or even a spoon of bagoong does the same job — a small amount of something salty and savory carries the whole dish.

Method

  1. Flake the tinapa off the bone, removing the skin and any large bones. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan or wok over medium-high heat. Sauté the garlic until fragrant and just starting to brown.
  3. Push the garlic to one side, crack in the eggs, and scramble until just set. Break into small pieces.
  4. Add the flaked tinapa and stir-fry for a minute to warm through and release its smokiness.
  5. Add the rice, breaking up any clumps with the back of your spoon. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until every grain is coated and slightly toasted.
  6. Season with soy sauce, toss in the spring onion, and stir-fry another minute. Serve with a squeeze of calamansi.
Make it stretch Day-old rice is not optional here — fresh rice turns mushy in the pan. If you're planning ahead, cook extra rice tonight specifically to fry tomorrow.
