Kangkong & Egg Stir-fry

Water spinach is one of the cheapest, fastest-cooking greens at any wet market. This is the quickest way to make a full plate of vegetables taste like it wasn't an afterthought.

Cost per serving ₱15
Total cost ₱30
Serves 2
Time 10 min

Ingredients

  • Kangkong (water spinach), 1 bunch1 bunch
  • Garlic, sliced4 cloves
  • Onion, sliced1/2
  • Egg2
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Oyster sauce or fish sauce1 tbsp
  • Ground black pepperto taste
Budget swap Any fast-cooking green works the same way — sweet potato tops, spinach, or pechay. Adjust cook time slightly; thicker leaves need another minute.

Method

  1. Wash the kangkong thoroughly and cut into 2-inch lengths, keeping the stems and leaves separate.
  2. Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Push everything to one side, crack in the eggs, and scramble briefly until just barely set — they'll finish cooking with the greens.
  4. Add the kangkong stems first and stir-fry for 1 minute, then add the leaves and stir-fry another 30–60 seconds, just until wilted. High heat and speed keep the color bright.
  5. Season with oyster or fish sauce and a crack of pepper. Toss everything together and serve immediately.
Make it stretch Add a sliced hot dog, leftover meat, or a spoon of ground pork if you have it — this dish is forgiving and takes well to whatever protein scrap needs using up.
